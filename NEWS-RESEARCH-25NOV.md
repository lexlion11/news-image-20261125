## 1. SPACE / SCIENCE

**Shenzhou-24 launches with the first foreign astronaut aboard the Chinese space station**
- Launched **08:18 CST** on 25 Nov 2026 from Jiuquan; separated ~**10 min** later into the planned orbit.
- Crew: commander **Tang Hongbo**, flight engineer **Wu Yi**, and Pakistani payload specialist **Muhammad Zeeshan Ali**.
- First foreign astronaut ever to visit Tiangong; first joint China–Pakistan crewed flight; marks **75 years** of China–Pakistan diplomatic relations.
- Source: Xinhua / CMSA (via CRI International Online)

**NASA completes assembly of the Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope**
- Inner and outer segments integrated **25 Nov** in the largest clean room at Goddard Space Flight Center, Greenbelt, Maryland.
- Wide-Field Instrument views are **100× Hubble's** field; first five years expected to characterise **100,000+ distant worlds**, hundreds of millions of stars, billions of galaxies.
- Launch by **May 2027** on a Falcon Heavy, on track for as early as **fall 2026**.
- Source: NASA

**Ariane 6 lofts 36 Amazon low-orbit satellites**
- Launched **24 Nov** from Kourou, French Guiana; deployed about **2 hours** later at roughly **460 km** altitude.
- **10th** Ariane 6 flight since its July 2024 maiden flight; **4th** Amazon Kuiper mission.
- Amazon has added **6** more Ariane 6 launches, bringing the contract to **24** missions; **136** Amazon satellites orbited by the company in 2026 alone.
- Source: Xinhua / Arianespace

**ESA's Hera reveals the first detailed post-impact images of asteroid Dimorphos**
- Media briefing **25 Nov, 11:00–14:00 CET**, Museum für Naturkunde Berlin; embargo lifts **18:30 CET**; public event ~**20:00 CET**.
- Follows NASA's DART impact of **Sept 2022**, which shortened Dimorphos's orbital period by about **33 minutes**.
- Hera's two CubeSats, **Milani** and **Juventas**, will follow with close-range measurements.
- Source: ESA

**NASA's SPHEREx maps the dust-obscured centre of the Milky Way**
- NASA's Astronomy Picture of the Day for **25 Nov 2026**: infrared data from SPHEREx, processed from NASA/IPAC IRSA archives.
- Near-infrared (~**1.2 microns**) is blocked by dust; mid-infrared light penetrates to reveal star-forming regions.
- The Milky Way is about **13 billion years** old.
- Source: NASA / APOD

**Uranus reaches opposition, its brightest of the year**
- Opposition at **17:00 CST** (18:00 EST) on **25 Nov 2026**; magnitude about **+5.6**, rising at sunset and visible all night.
- It sits in front of Taurus, next to the Pleiades; binoculars needed.
- Venus reaches peak brightness (**magnitude −4.87**) on **27 Nov**.
- Source: EarthSky via Starlust
