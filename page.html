Space JIUQUAN, CHINA OFFICIAL

Shenzhou-24 lifts off carrying the first foreign astronaut to Tiangong

China's crewed Shenzhou-24 launched at 08:18 CST from Jiuquan for the Tiangong space station. Commander Tang Hongbo and flight engineer Wu Yi fly with Pakistani payload specialist Muhammad Zeeshan Ali - the first foreign astronaut to visit the station and the first China-Pakistan joint flight, timed to the 75th anniversary of diplomatic ties between the two countries.
